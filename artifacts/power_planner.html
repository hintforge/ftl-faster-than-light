FTL: Faster Than Light -- Power Allocation Scratchpad

Plan your reactor power budget by hand. No account, no internet connection, no AI required -- this page only adds up numbers you type in.

Vanilla Advanced Edition figures only. Weapons and Drone Control power draw depends on your loadout and is not modeled. Sources and limitations are embedded in this file (see the hf-manifest data block in its source). This is one prototype tool, not a full FTL companion app.
