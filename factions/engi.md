## Current status
Usually neutral/friendly faction of repair-focused robots. Distinct from the playable Engi crew species (`crew/engi.md`).

## Recruitment / Access (unlock event)
- Engi **Homeworlds** holds the unique **Engi fleet discussion** event which, with an Engi crewmember aboard, unlocks the **Stealth Cruiser** -- this is in Homeworlds, not the Engi Controlled sector. *(spoiler: progression.)*
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `../dependencies.md` SEQ-001: Engi crew + Engi Homeworlds (not Engi Controlled) are both required to trigger the Stealth Cruiser unlock.

## Combat / disposition exception
> `spoiler: progression` · enemy-tier 1 (gated; available on request).
- Engi are usually neutral, **but hostile Engi events exist**: poorly-equipped Engi ships that attack on seeing Federation markings, and Engi-virus events that spawn Defense Drone II ships. This is the documented exception to "Engi are always neutral."
