## Combat / disposition
> `spoiler: progression` · enemy-tier 1 (gated; available on request).
- Mantis ships favor boarding (teleport crew aboard you). They **often carry an Engi (slave) crew**; the enemy AI may teleport the Engi onto your ship and leave the Mantis on repair.
- Counter-play (build advice): door upgrades + Mantis/Rock defenders; vent rooms to space to defeat boarders. Weak against fragile crews (Zoltan).
