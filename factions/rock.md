## Current status
Stoic, tanky faction; mostly neutral with some hostile encounters. Distinct from the playable Rock crew species (`crew/rock.md`).

## Recruitment / Access & gated content
- Rock **Homeworlds** holds the **Ancient device** (Crystal chain step 3): with Ruwen alive, a blue option teleports you to the Hidden Crystal Worlds. Once per game, sector 5+ only -- the hard bottleneck of the Crystal unlock. *(spoiler: late-game.)* See `crew/crystal.md`.
- The Rock Cruiser quest (survive a solar flare, escort) unlocks the Rock ship. See `items/builds.md`.
