## Current status
Mostly peaceful energy-being faction; hostile in their Controlled sectors despite the green (supposedly safe) map color. Distinct from the playable Zoltan crew species (`crew/zoltan.md`).

## Recruitment / Access & gated content
- The Zoltan **peace event** (hail, choose "without war" then "no bloodshed") unlocks the **Zoltan Cruiser**. *(spoiler: progression.)* See `items/builds.md`.
- Zoltan sectors host the **Zoltan research facility** (Crystal chain step 2; revives Ruwen if you carry the Damaged Stasis Pod). Zoltan Homeworlds can be required for some Crystal routing. *(spoiler: progression.)* See `crew/crystal.md`.

## Combat / disposition
> `spoiler: progression` · enemy-tier 1 (gated; available on request).
- Enemy Zoltan ships carry **Zoltan Super Shields** (5 HP, impenetrable by everything -- including boarding, missiles, and bombs -- until depleted). Zoltan (green) sectors are among the most dangerous despite the map color.
- Counter-play: bring shield-piercers (missiles/bombs/ion) or boarding to beat the Super Shield.
