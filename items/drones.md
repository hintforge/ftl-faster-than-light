# FTL: Faster Than Light -- Drones

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

Drones are deployed via the Drone Control system and consume Drone Parts. Values are vanilla Advanced Edition.

## Defense drones
- **Defense Drone Mk I** (50 scrap, 2 power): shoots down missiles, asteroids, flak debris, hacking drones, boarding drones, ion intruders, and crystal shots -- the single strongest reason to buy Drone Control.
- **Defense Drone Mk II** (70 scrap, 3 power): adds lasers and ions to its interception list, but is often a downgrade -- it gets distracted by low-threat projectiles and lets missiles through.

## Combat & offensive drones
- **Combat Drone Mk I / Mk II** -- attack enemy hull/systems automatically; ignore shields' targeting but can be shot down by enemy defense drones.
- **Beam Drone (AE)** -- slow-sweeping beam that damages multiple rooms.
- **Boarding Drone** -- breaches the hull and attacks crew/systems from inside; killed by a Zoltan Super Shield; **cannot board a cloaked ship.**
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `../dependencies.md` DEP-005: Neither Boarding Drone nor Teleporter can act on a cloaked ship; Cloaking provides full boarding immunity while active.
- **Ion Intruder** -- boards and ionizes enemy systems from within.

## Support drones
- **Hull Repair Drone** / **System Repair Drone** -- repair your own hull or systems.
- **Anti-Combat Drone (AE)** -- stuns enemy combat/hacking/boarding drones; 47.8% chance to destroy a target over 5s.
- **Shield Overcharger / Zoltan Shield Drone** -- periodically adds Zoltan-shield layers to your ship.

## Drone economy
- Deploying any drone and launching a hacking drone consumes Drone Parts. The **Drone Recovery Arm** augment recovers external drones (jump after 2 repairs). Internal drones (boarding/ion intruders) are not recovered.
