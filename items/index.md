## Resources (tracked as run economy, not acquirable items)

- **Scrap** -- primary currency; buys and upgrades everything at stores
- **Missiles** -- ammo for missile/bomb weapons; finite per run; restocked at stores
- **Drone Parts** -- consumed deploying drones; finite per run; restocked at stores
- **Fuel** -- required for FTL jumps; 0 fuel = stranded ("Wait"); restocked at beacons/stores

These are documented in `mechanics.md` under "Run resources" and "Scrap & economy."
