# FTL: Faster Than Light -- Weapons

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

All values are **vanilla Advanced Edition**. Power = reactor bars; Charge = cooldown (s); Pierce = shield layers ignored. Fire chance rolls before breach (a shot can't do both). Crew damage = system damage ×15 HP per occupant. A popular balance MOD changes Heavy Laser I to 2 power / 10s charge -- do not conflate with these vanilla values.

## Weapon acquisition

Weapons drop from store beacons (price = scrap value above), event rewards, and auto-ship/elite-ship victories. Ship-only weapons (listed "ship-only") come pre-installed on specific starting layouts and are not sold. Crystal weapons appear only via the Crystal-sector content above.
