# FTL: Faster Than Light -- Settings

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

Platform: PC / Steam. FTL's run modifiers are limited to **difficulty** + the **Advanced Edition toggle** -- there are no seeds or mutators in vanilla.

> Exact in-game menu tab names were not confirmed in the P1 result; the headings below organize by topic. Treat tab labels as unverified.

## Difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard)

_source: P1 deep-research cascade 2026-06-02 (ftl.fandom.com + community guides) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

- Three difficulties; **Hard was added with Advanced Edition.**
- **Lower difficulty gives MORE scrap per reward** (scrap scales by sector × difficulty); resource rewards (fuel/missiles/drone parts) are fixed. Enemy hull/systems scale by sector and difficulty.
- **Easy:** the Anti-Ship Battery (ASB) never hits exit beacons.
- **Hard:** having >25% evasion puts your Piloting/Engines on the enemy's high-priority target list.
- No achievements are difficulty-locked except the two victory achievements: "Federation Victory (Easy)" requires beating the Flagship on Easy or higher; "Federation Victory (Normal)" requires Normal or higher.
- [Confirmed: 3 sources]

> A Flagship-specific difficulty detail (3 vs 4 shield layers on Easy+AE-off; Hard's 2 extra rooms) is `spoiler: late-game` and lives in `mechanics.md` → "The Rebel Flagship (final boss)."

## Advanced Edition toggle

_source: P1 deep-research cascade 2026-06-02 (ftl.fandom.com + Steam) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

- **Advanced Edition** is a free update, **on by default**, toggled from the main menu. It is not paid DLC.
- Disabling AE removes: **Lanius** crew/ships, **Clone Bay**, **Hacking**, **Mind Control**, **Beam Drone** and **Anti-Combat Drone**, **Backup Battery**, the **Abandoned Sector**, AE-only events, and all **Type-C** ship layouts.
- Disabling AE also triggers a **store-slot bug** (frequent empty store slots).
- Recommendation: leave AE on unless deliberately avoiding the new systems.
- [Confirmed]
